Hangs — three design directions

A Hang is a lightweight, real-world meetup a user creates and invites friends & mutuals to. It lives in the feed, drops a pin on the map, and lets people RSVP “Going”. Below: the three surfaces — the map marker, the feed card, and the detail view — each rendered in three distinct visual directions. Same signature accent across all three so the directions differ in style, not color.

①

Quiet

Minimal & type-led. White surfaces, hairline borders, generous whitespace. Coral appears only where it earns attention — the Hang tag and the RSVP action. Closest to the existing PostCard, refined.
